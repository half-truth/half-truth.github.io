> Growth and distribution are not determined by what you are best at, but by the step you must perform yet is hardest to automate.  
> 100% automation and 99.8% automation are not a trivial ‘0.2 percentage point’ difference; they are two different worlds.  
> The power of AI lies not in ‘how much it can do,’ but in ‘which segment it replaces, which remaining segment becomes the bottleneck, and whether that final 0.2% can be replaced.  
>
> \- [Artificial Intelligence and Economic Growth](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U-ziizXkuXg)

> Drive any single link's efficiency to infinity, and overall output rises by roughly that link's share of total output.  
> Heavy investment with slow visible returns is normal — not a signal of failure.  
> The same chain rises slowly and falls fast. … Be patient on the upside, urgent on the downside — these two must be done at once.  
>
> \- [“AI and Our Economic Future” with Professor Chad Jones](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xBpGn3BDcOY)
